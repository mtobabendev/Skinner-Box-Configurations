# With Extra Steps Protocol

## Definition

A normal task burdened by extra, unnecessary steps imposed by an organization, platform, device, application, account system, or support tool.

The protocol treats friction as evidence rather than background inconvenience.

## Record for every task

1. The original task and the shortest reasonable path.
2. The actual path imposed on the user.
3. Every additional click, command, download, installation, transfer, device, account, login, correction, retry, and workaround.
4. Elapsed time for the ordinary task and the imposed process.
5. Download size, storage consumed, bandwidth used, and equipment required when measurable.
6. Errors, blocked functions, misleading instructions, delays, and abandoned paths.
7. Emotional, accessibility, financial, and practical costs.
8. The responsible organization or system for each added step.
9. The eventual successful human-selected workaround.
10. The difference between the expected and actual burden.

## ChatGPT-specific rule

Every ChatGPT mistake, omission, unnecessary recommendation, correction cycle, withholding, redaction, or scope error must be recorded in `chatgpt-intervention-log.csv`, including the resulting extra steps and time cost.

## Working phrase

> The normal task—with extra unnecessary steps.
